Changes: retained 8,920 rows with visual magnitude <= 6.5, excluded the Sun, selected/reordered fields, sorted by brightness and converted CSV to JSON. No synthetic star records were added. Source URL, fixed commit, original CSV checksum, epoch, units and field ordering are recorded in stars-hyg-v41.json and the project ASTRONOMY.md. Coordinate positions are parsecs and velocities are parsecs/year in the J2000 equatorial frame.
